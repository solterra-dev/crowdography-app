- 0.1.0 build 8: Chronum address corrected to chronumapi.com.

- 0.1.0 build 17: the app opens on Your footage, not the camera. Your stored videos and photos are listed with preview pictures and play in the app, on any phone you log in on. Film opens the camera; the X closes it. Account menu: join a gathering, uploads, sign out. Needs server 0.11.
